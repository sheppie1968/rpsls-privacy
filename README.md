# Privacy Policy for Rock Paper Scissors Lizard Spock

**Effective date:** 31 August 2026
**Last updated:** 31 August 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android application **Rock Paper Scissors Lizard Spock**
(package name `com.rockpaperscissors.lizardspock`, the "App"), published by Mark Shepherd
(the "Developer", "we", "us").

## Summary

The App does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal or device data. It works
entirely offline on your device and has no internet access.

## Data we collect

**None.** The App does not collect any user data or device data.

Specifically, the App does **not** collect, request, or access:

- Your name, email address, or any other contact or identity information
- Location data of any kind, including approximate or precise location
- Photos, videos, audio, files, or documents on your device
- Contacts, calendar entries, call logs, SMS messages, or other messages
- Device or advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
- Health, fitness, or financial information
- App activity, browsing history, search history, or usage analytics
- Crash logs or diagnostic reports

## Permissions

The App requests **no Android runtime permissions**. It does not hold the `INTERNET`
permission, so it is technically incapable of sending information off your device.

## Information you enter into the App

When you set up a game, you may type player names (for example, "Alice" or "Player 2").
These names are used only to label players on screen during the current game session.

- They are held only in your device's temporary memory (RAM).
- They are never written to a database, file, or preferences store by the App.
- They are never transmitted anywhere.
- They are discarded when you close the App or start a new game.

We recommend using nicknames rather than full legal names, as you would with any game.

## Third-party services

The App contains **no** advertising networks, analytics services, crash-reporting tools,
social media SDKs, or other third-party data-collection libraries. It uses only Google's
standard AndroidX and Jetpack Compose user-interface libraries, which run locally on your
device and do not collect data on our behalf.

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not have any data to share.

## Android system backup

The App uses the Android platform's standard settings. Because the App does not save any
data to your device, there is nothing meaningful for Android's backup or device-transfer
features to include. Any use of Android Backup is controlled by your own device and Google
account settings, and is governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), not by us.

## Children's privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences, including children. Because we collect no data
whatsoever, we do not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children
under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We comply with the Children's
Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policies by collecting
nothing at all.

## Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK) and the CCPA (California) give you rights to
access, correct, delete, and port your personal data, and to object to its sale. We hold no
personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, disclose, correct, delete, or
sell. We do not sell or share personal information under any definition.

To remove the player names you have typed, simply close the App.

## Data security

Because no data leaves your device and none is stored, there is no data for us to secure,
transmit, or expose in a breach.

## Purchases

The App is free and contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions. We do not process
payments or handle any financial information.

## Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects privacy — for example, if a future version adds
online multiplayer, statistics saving, or advertising — we will update this policy and
change the "Last updated" date above before releasing that version. Please review this page
periodically.
