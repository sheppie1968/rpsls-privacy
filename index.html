Privacy Policy

Rock Paper Scissors Lizard Spock
Effective date: 31 August 2026 · Last updated: 19 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android application Rock Paper Scissors Lizard Spock (package name com.rockpaperscissors.lizardspock, the “App”), published by Mark Shepherd (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”).

Summary: The App does not collect, share, or transmit any personal or device data. It works entirely offline on your device and has no internet access. To save retyping, it remembers the player names you use and a simple win record for each, stored only on your device and deletable at any time.

Data we collect

None. The App does not collect any user data or device data.

Specifically, the App does not collect, request, or access:

  • Your name, email address, or any other contact or identity information
  • Location data of any kind, including approximate or precise location
  • Photos, videos, audio, files, or documents on your device
  • Contacts, calendar entries, call logs, SMS messages, or other messages
  • Device or advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • Health, fitness, or financial information
  • App activity, browsing history, search history, or usage analytics
  • Crash logs or diagnostic reports

Permissions

The App requests no Android runtime permissions. It does not hold the INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending information off your device.

Information you enter into the App

When you set up a game, you may type player names (for example, “Alice” or “Player 2”) and, optionally, what the winner gets to do (for example, “pick the restaurant”).

Remembered players. So you don’t have to retype your group each time, the App saves:

  • the game mode and players from your last game, and
  • for each player name, how many games they have played and won, and when they last played (up to 30 names).

This information:

  • is stored in a private file inside the App’s own storage on your device, which other apps cannot read;
  • is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it never leaves your device;
  • is never transmitted anywhere, and we (the Developer) never receive it;
  • can be deleted at any time — see “Your rights” below.

Everything else you type, including what the winner gets to do, is used only for the current game. While a game is in progress, Android may temporarily keep it so the game survives the App being paused in the background; it is discarded when you close the App.

We recommend using nicknames rather than full legal names, as you would with any game.

Third-party services

The App contains no advertising networks, analytics services, crash-reporting tools, social media SDKs, or other third-party data-collection libraries. It uses only Google’s standard AndroidX and Jetpack Compose user-interface libraries, which run locally on your device and do not collect data on our behalf.

We do not share data with third parties, because we do not have any data to share.

Android system backup

The App explicitly excludes its remembered players from Android’s cloud backup and device-transfer features, and saves nothing else for them to include. Any use of Android Backup is controlled by your own device and Google account settings, and is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy, not by us.

Children’s privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences, including children. Because we collect no data whatsoever, we do not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play’s Families policies by collecting nothing at all.

Your rights

Data-protection laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK) and the CCPA (California) give you rights to access, correct, delete, and port your personal data, and to object to its sale. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, disclose, correct, delete, or sell. We do not sell or share personal information under any definition.

To delete remembered players and their win records, tap Forget under “Recent Players” on the App’s setup screen. Clearing the App’s storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the App, also deletes them.

Data security

Because no data leaves your device, there is no data for us to secure, transmit, or expose in a breach. Remembered players are kept in the App’s private storage, protected by Android’s app sandbox.

Purchases

The App is free and contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions. We do not process payments or handle any financial information.

Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects privacy — for example, if a future version adds online multiplayer, cloud sync, or advertising — we will update this policy and change the “Last updated” date above before releasing that version. Please review this page periodically.
